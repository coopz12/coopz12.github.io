About this flight

STRATA is a small flight exhibit. A glider follows a single curve of sky from the ground haze to the thin blue above it, and ten facts from the world of aviation are strung along the way like markers on a chart. There is no destination and no score. Scroll slowly when a fact drifts into view and the flight will slow down with you, the way you might lean toward a label in a quiet gallery.

Built with three.js and set in Playfair Display and DM Sans. All facts are written from general aviation knowledge; their sources are listed at the end of the flight. Every model on this page, the glider, the clouds and the balloon, was made procedurally for this exhibit.

Thank you for flying

You have reached the top of the climb, where the blue gives out and the facts run out with it. If you ride it again, ride it slower. The clouds are never in quite the same mood twice.
